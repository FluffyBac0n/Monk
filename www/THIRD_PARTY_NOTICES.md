
## Material Design fit-screen icon

The fit-screen SVG in `components/FitTrailButton.tsx` is converted from the
`fit_screen_rounded` glyph in the Google Material Icons font bundled with Flutter,
the same icon used by EuroTrex mobile. Copyright Google. Licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The glyph outline is unchanged; its coordinate orientation is adapted for SVG.
